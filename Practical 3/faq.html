How do I register on StudentHub?

You can register by visiting the Register page and filling out the form with your details.

How do I log in?

Go to the Login page, enter your email and password, and select your role (Student/Admin).

Who can access the Admin Dashboard?

Only users with admin roles can access the Admin Dashboard.

How can I contact support?

Visit the Contact page and use the form to send a message to the support team.
